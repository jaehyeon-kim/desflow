# Building Blocks

A model is made of a few kinds of parts. An **entity** is the thing that flows through it: an order, a visitor, a support call. It enters through an arrival, does the work its task describes, and holds a resource while it works. [What Is DES?](https://jaehyeon.me/desflow/latest/des/how-it-works/index.md) explains the simulation they run in.

| Block            | What it models                                                  | YAML         | Builder                            | Low-level                                     | Page                                                                                                       |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Arrival          | how often an entity enters                                      | `arrivals`   | `add_arrival`, `@app.arrival_loop` | a process that waits for a drawn gap          | [Arrivals and Services](https://jaehyeon.me/desflow/latest/building-blocks/arrivals-and-services/index.md) |
| Service          | how long a piece of work takes                                  | `services`   | `add_service`                      | a `DistributionConfig` drawn with a `Sampler` | [Arrivals and Services](https://jaehyeon.me/desflow/latest/building-blocks/arrivals-and-services/index.md) |
| Task             | what one entity does, with its events                           | `tasks`      | `@app.task`                        | a SimPy process                               | [Tasks and Processes](https://jaehyeon.me/desflow/latest/building-blocks/tasks-and-processes/index.md)     |
| Resource         | packers, benches, lanes: whole units held by a task             | `resources`  | `add_resource`                     | `DynamicResource`                             | [Resources](https://jaehyeon.me/desflow/latest/building-blocks/resources/index.md)                         |
| Container, store | a quantity, such as a stock of boxes, or a collection of items  | `containers` | `add_container`                    | `DynamicContainer`, `DynamicStore`            | [Resources](https://jaehyeon.me/desflow/latest/building-blocks/resources/index.md)                         |
| Process          | anything else that runs over time: a break, a drift, a schedule | `processes`  | `add_process`                      | `env.process`                                 | [Tasks and Processes](https://jaehyeon.me/desflow/latest/building-blocks/tasks-and-processes/index.md)     |
| Variable         | any other live value, such as a speed                           | `variables`  | `add_variable`                     | `SimParameter(variables=...)`                 | [Live Parameters](https://jaehyeon.me/desflow/latest/architecture/registry/index.md)                       |

[First Simulation](https://jaehyeon.me/desflow/latest/tutorials/first-simulation/index.md) puts the first four together into a small online shop.
