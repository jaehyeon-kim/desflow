    Statistic Names

    Every name the built-in statistics publish, at each level, generated by running the statistics in the code. Statistics explains the levels and how each value is computed. Each level publishes the names of the levels before it, and the new ones listed under it. <sim_id> is the simulation's id, <name> a resource, store or container, <task> a task, and <arrival> the arrival that started a flow's entities.

    none

    Publishes nothing: the statistics are off.

    minimal

    Name Measures
    <sim_id>.stats.resources.<name>.capacity units now
    <sim_id>.stats.resources.<name>.in_use units held by tasks; above capacity after a drop, until they finish
    <sim_id>.stats.resources.<name>.queue_length requests waiting now
    <sim_id>.stats.resources.<name>.utilization in_use as a percentage of capacity, now
    <sim_id>.stats.resources.<name>.down 1 while a resource with a breakdown is down, 0 otherwise
    <sim_id>.stats.stores.<name>.capacity items it can hold now
    <sim_id>.stats.stores.<name>.level items held now
    <sim_id>.stats.stores.<name>.getters_waiting tasks waiting to get an item
    <sim_id>.stats.stores.<name>.putters_waiting tasks waiting to put an item
    <sim_id>.stats.containers.<name>.capacity largest amount, now
    <sim_id>.stats.containers.<name>.level amount held now
    <sim_id>.stats.containers.<name>.getters_waiting tasks waiting to take an amount
    <sim_id>.stats.containers.<name>.putters_waiting tasks waiting to add an amount
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.in_progress instances queued or started and not yet ended, now

    basic

    Name Measures
    <sim_id>.stats.resources.<name>.utilization_avg utilisation averaged over time, in percent
    <sim_id>.stats.resources.<name>.queue_length_avg requests waiting, averaged over time
    <sim_id>.stats.resources.<name>.downtime seconds a resource with a breakdown has been down so far
    <sim_id>.stats.resources.<name>.failures failures of a resource with a breakdown so far
    <sim_id>.stats.stores.<name>.level_avg items held, averaged over time
    <sim_id>.stats.containers.<name>.level_avg amount held, averaged over time
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.finished instances finished so far
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.balked instances that refused to join the queue; published once one does
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.reneged instances that left the queue before they were served; published once one does
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.failed instances that failed: their fail held, their function raised or their wait failed; published once one does
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.interrupted interrupts delivered to instances, preemptions not included; published once one is
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.preempted times a higher-priority request took an instance's unit; published once one does
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.resumed times an interrupted instance got its units back and was served again; published once one does
    <sim_id>.stats.flows.<arrival>.finished entities that left the model, by the arrival that started them
    <sim_id>.stats.flows.<arrival>.started entities whose first task has started, by the arrival that started them
    <sim_id>.stats.flows.<arrival>.in_system entities in the model now: started, and not yet finished, failed or gone
    <sim_id>.stats.flows.<arrival>.failed entities whose flow ended in a failure no on_fail handled; published once one does

    typical

    Name Measures
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.wait_mean mean time from queued to started
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.wait_max longest time from queued to started
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.service_mean mean time from started to finished
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.service_max longest time from started to finished

    all

    Name Measures
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.wait_p50 median wait
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.wait_p95 the wait 95% of instances stay under
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.wait_p99 the wait 99% of instances stay under
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.service_p50 median service time
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.service_p95 the service time 95% of instances stay under
    <sim_id>.stats.tasks.<task>.service_p99 the service time 99% of instances stay under
